HOW SOURCES COMBINE

Every date-typed column can be marked "Join column" — do this once per source. The join anchor's join column sets the master date sequence that appears on the dashboard; every other source's join column is matched against those dates by value, not by name, so it's fine if one sheet calls it "Date" and another calls it "Day" or "Timestamp." Missing days show as blank, not dropped.
